## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Nexora", "the Company") plans, approves, incurs, and reimburses business travel and other business expenses. Its aims are to make sure that employees travel safely and comfortably, that money is spent prudently and transparently, and that the Company complies with the Income-tax Act, 1961, the Goods and Services Tax laws, and the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999.

The policy is issued by the Finance department under Section 8.6 of the Human Resources Policy Manual (NXR-HR-001). It must be read with the Information Technology Security Policy (NXR-IT-001), which sets rules for carrying and using Company devices while travelling, and with the Code of Conduct and Ethics Policy (NXR-COC-001), which governs gifts, hospitality, and honest reporting of expenses.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This policy applies to all Nexora employees, including employees on probation, at all offices in Pune, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad.

2.2 It applies to contractors and interns only where their contract or engagement letter provides for reimbursement of expenses, and then on the same terms, in line with Section 14.4 of NXR-HR-001. In particular, travel or expenditure above ₹25,000 for a contractor requires the same Department Head and Finance approvals as for an employee.

2.3 It covers domestic travel, international travel, local conveyance, accommodation, meals, communication, client entertainment, and incidental business expenses. It does not cover salary advances, loans, or expenses that the Company pays directly through purchase orders under the Procurement Policy.

2.4 Working remotely from another city or another country at the employee's own request is **not** business travel. Costs of such arrangements are governed by Sections 7.4 and 8.6 of the Remote Work and Hybrid Working Policy (NXR-HR-002), and are not reimbursable under this policy.

## 3. Principles and Definitions

3.1 **Business need.** Travel must be necessary, and must be for a purpose that cannot reasonably be met by a video call or other alternative. Managers must challenge travel that has no clear business objective.

3.2 **Prudence.** Employees are expected to spend Company money as they would spend their own, choosing reasonable, cost-effective options.

3.3 **Honesty.** Every claim must be accurate and supported. Falsifying or inflating a claim, claiming personal expenses as business expenses, or submitting a claim for an expense that was not incurred is fraud and gross misconduct under Section 11.4 of NXR-HR-001 and Section 13 of NXR-COC-001.

3.4 **Definitions.**

- **Trip cost:** the total estimated cost of a business trip, including fares, accommodation, meals, local conveyance, and other expenses, expressed in Indian rupees.
- **Department Head:** the Director (D1) or Vice President responsible for the employee's department, as defined in Section 3.3 of NXR-HR-001.
- **Finance approver:** the Finance Controller, or a Finance business partner authorised in writing by the CFO.
- **Tier 1 cities:** Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, and Pune.
- **Tier 2 cities:** all other cities and towns in India.
- **NexoraExpense:** the Company's online system for travel requests, advances, and claims.

## 4. Approval Matrix

4.1 **Prior approval is required.** All business travel and any single planned expense above ₹5,000 must be approved before it is incurred, using a travel request or expense request in NexoraExpense. Approval after the event is granted only in genuine emergencies and requires an explanation.

4.2 **Approval levels.** The required approval depends on the total trip cost or, for a single non-travel expense, the total amount of the expense:

| Total Trip Cost / Expense (₹) | Required Approval |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹25,000 | Reporting manager |
| **Above ₹25,000 and up to ₹1,00,000** | **Department Head and Finance approver** |
| Above ₹1,00,000 and up to ₹5,00,000 | Department Head and CFO |
| Above ₹5,00,000 | CEO and CFO |
| Any international trip (regardless of amount) | Department Head and CFO |

4.3 **Explanation of the ₹25,000 threshold.** Any travel or expense that exceeds **₹25,000** requires the approval of **both** the Department Head and the Finance approver. Approval by only one of them is not sufficient, and the reporting manager's approval is required in addition to the approvals above. The threshold applies to the total cost of a single trip, or to a single expense, and must not be avoided by splitting a trip or purchase into several smaller requests. Finance monitors for split transactions and reports them to the CFO under Section 13.

4.4 **Approvals for senior roles.** Nobody may approve their own travel or expense. The following applies:

- Employees at L1 to L5: the reporting manager, and above ₹25,000, the Department Head and Finance approver.
- Managers (M1 and M2): the Department Head for all trips, together with the Finance approver above ₹25,000.
- Department Heads (D1 and VP): the CFO, or, for Finance, the CEO.
- The CFO: the CEO.
- The CEO: the Chair of the Audit Committee of the Board.

4.5 **Approval time.** Approvers should act on requests within 3 working days. If an approver does not respond within 5 working days, the employee may escalate to the approver's manager or to Finance.

4.6 **Validity.** An approval is valid for the dates, destination, and cost stated. If the trip cost increases by more than 10 percent, or crosses a threshold in Section 4.2, the employee must submit a revised request before booking further items.

4.7 **Employees on probation.** Employees on probation under Section 5 of NXR-HR-001 may be approved for business travel in the same way as confirmed employees. Approval thresholds are identical.

4.8 **Budget.** Approval of a request confirms business need and reasonableness but does not replace budget control. Department Heads are responsible for ensuring that travel remains within their approved departmental budget.

## 5. Booking Travel

5.1 **Approved channels.** All flights, trains, and hotels must be booked through the Company's approved travel management company (TMC) accessible from NexoraExpense, unless a booking through the TMC is impossible and Finance has agreed to an alternative in advance.

5.2 **Advance booking.** Domestic travel should be booked at least 7 days before departure, and international travel at least 21 days before departure. Employees who fail to book in advance without a good reason may be asked to bear the extra cost above the lowest available fare.

5.3 **Air travel entitlements.**

| Travel Type | L1 – L5 | M1 – M2 | D1 and above |
|---|---|---|---|
| Domestic flights | Economy | Economy | Economy (premium economy where offered as a standard fare) |
| International flights up to 8 hours | Economy | Economy | Economy |
| International flights over 8 hours | Economy | Premium economy | Business class, with CFO approval |

5.4 **Lowest logical fare.** Employees must choose the lowest logical fare within a departure window of two hours before or after the preferred time. Refundable and flexible fares may be booked where the trip is likely to change.

5.5 **Rail travel.** Employees may travel by train in AC 2-tier (2A) class. Employees at D1 grade and above may travel in AC 1st class (1A). Executive chair car is permitted for day journeys on Vande Bharat and Shatabdi services.

5.6 **Road travel.** For journeys under 300 km, road travel by a Company-approved cab or by the employee's own vehicle may be more economical than air travel. Where an employee uses a personal vehicle for business, mileage is reimbursed under Section 8.3.

5.7 **Cancellations.** Employees must cancel unused bookings through the TMC as soon as the plan changes. Cancellation charges arising from a change of plan for personal reasons are borne by the employee.

5.8 **Personal travel added to a business trip.** Personal days added to a business trip are permitted with the reporting manager's approval. The Company will pay only the cost that it would have incurred for the business portion of the trip. Personal leave is applied for under Section 7 of NXR-HR-001. Employees must not claim accommodation, meals, or local conveyance for personal days.

## 6. Accommodation

6.1 **Approved hotels.** Employees should stay in hotels included in the TMC's corporate rate list. Where none is available, the employee may book any reasonable business hotel within the limits below.

6.2 **Nightly limits.** The maximum room rate per night, excluding taxes, is:

| Grade | Tier 1 Cities | Tier 2 Cities |
|---|---|---|
| L1 – L5 | ₹7,000 | ₹4,500 |
| M1 – M2 | ₹9,000 | ₹6,000 |
| D1 and above | ₹12,000 | ₹8,000 |

6.3 **Exceeding the limit.** A room rate above the limit requires the advance approval of the Department Head and the Finance approver, for example during a major conference when hotels are sold out. Without approval, the excess is borne by the employee.

6.4 **Stay with friends or relatives.** Employees who stay with friends or relatives are not entitled to accommodation reimbursement, but may claim a lodging allowance of ₹1,500 per night without receipts.

6.5 **Extended stays.** For stays of more than 21 consecutive days at one location, Finance may authorise serviced apartments at a rate that is lower than the equivalent cumulative hotel cost.

6.6 **Security when staying in hotels.** Employees must follow the travel security requirements in Section 7 of NXR-IT-001, including keeping laptops locked, never leaving Company devices in hotel rooms unattended without using the in-room safe, and using NexoraVPN on hotel and public networks.

## 7. Meals and Per Diem

7.1 **Daily meal limits (domestic).** While on approved overnight business travel, employees may claim actual meal expenses up to the following daily caps, inclusive of taxes and reasonable service charges:

| Grade | Tier 1 Cities | Tier 2 Cities |
|---|---|---|
| L1 – L5 | ₹2,500 per day | ₹1,800 per day |
| M1 – M2 | ₹3,500 per day | ₹2,500 per day |
| D1 and above | ₹4,500 per day | ₹3,000 per day |

7.2 **Meals included.** If a meal is provided by the hotel, host, client, or conference, or is included in the ticket, the employee may not claim that meal. The daily cap is reduced by ₹500 for breakfast, ₹800 for lunch, and ₹1,000 for dinner where these are provided.

7.3 **Same-day travel.** For day trips of more than 8 hours away from the home office, a lunch allowance of up to ₹700 may be claimed with a receipt.

7.4 **Alcohol.** Alcohol, tobacco, and personal entertainment are not reimbursable.

7.5 **Team meals.** Where a meal is shared by a team while travelling, the most senior employee present should pay and claim, listing all attendees. Each attendee's share must not exceed the applicable daily cap.

## 8. Local Conveyance

8.1 **Local transport.** Employees may use metro, buses, auto-rickshaws, and app-based cabs for business travel within a city. Economy or standard category cab service must be selected. Receipts must be attached for each ride above ₹500.

8.2 **Airport and railway transfers.** Cab transfers between an airport or railway station and the home, office, or hotel are reimbursable. For journeys to airports at Pune, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad from the respective offices, the cost should be approved as part of the trip request.

8.3 **Personal vehicle mileage.** When an employee uses a personal vehicle for approved business travel, mileage is reimbursed at **₹12 per km** for cars and **₹5 per km** for two-wheelers, with a trip log showing dates, start and end points, and odometer readings. Fuel, parking, and toll costs are covered by the mileage rate, except that tolls may be claimed separately with receipts. The Company does not cover fines, repairs, or insurance excess.

8.4 **Commuting.** Daily commuting between home and the regular place of work is a personal expense and is not reimbursable. Travel between Nexora offices at the Company's request is business travel.

8.5 **Rentals.** Car hire may be approved by the Department Head where it is the most cost-effective option for a group or for multiple locations.

## 9. International Travel

9.1 **Approval.** All international business travel, regardless of cost, requires the prior approval of the **Department Head** and the **CFO**. Where the total trip cost exceeds ₹5,00,000, the CEO's approval is also required, as stated in Section 4.2.

9.2 **Documents.** Employees are responsible for holding a valid passport and visa. The Company will pay for visa fees, and for expedited visa services that are needed because of business urgency, provided that they are approved in the trip request. Employees must not travel on a visa type that does not permit business activity.

9.3 **Foreign exchange.** Forex cards or foreign currency for travel must be obtained through the Company's approved forex provider or the TMC and, where an advance is taken, in line with Section 10. Claims for expenses in foreign currency are reimbursed in rupees at the actual rate incurred, or at the Company's month-end rate if the actual rate is not available.

9.4 **Per diem for international travel.** International per diem, in place of meal, incidental, and local transport claims, is USD 80 per day for Tier A countries (the United States, the United Kingdom, the Eurozone countries, Switzerland, Singapore, Japan, and Australia) and USD 60 per day for all other countries. Accommodation is booked through the TMC at reasonable business hotel rates approved with the trip request.

9.5 **Travel insurance.** The Company purchases travel insurance for all approved international trips. Employees must carry the policy details with them.

9.6 **Security and devices.** Employees who travel internationally with Company devices must follow Section 7 of NXR-IT-001. Travel to countries designated as high risk by the CISO requires the use of a loaner device with limited data, which is arranged by IT & Security at least 5 working days before departure. Devices must not be left in checked baggage.

9.7 **Work from abroad.** Where an employee requests to work from abroad for personal reasons, the rules in Section 8 of NXR-HR-002 apply and this policy does not. Where the Company asks an employee to work abroad for a longer period, the arrangement is treated as international business travel under this section and may also require a relocation agreement.

9.8 **Client and conference travel.** Registration fees for conferences and trade shows may be reimbursed if they are approved in advance. Where the fee exceeds ₹25,000, approvals are required as in Section 4.2.

## 10. Advances

10.1 **Eligibility.** Confirmed employees may request an advance for approved travel of at least 2 nights. Employees who are on probation may request an advance only with the additional approval of the Finance approver. Interns and contractors are not entitled to advances.

10.2 **Amount.** The advance may be up to **80 percent** of the estimated approved cost of the trip, excluding items already booked through the TMC. The advance is paid to the employee's registered bank account at least 2 working days before departure.

10.3 **Settlement.** The employee must submit the claim within **15 calendar days** of returning from the trip and settle the advance against actual expenses. Any excess advance must be refunded through bank transfer within the same period.

10.4 **Recovery.** If the advance is not settled within 30 days after return, Finance may recover it from the employee's salary after written notice. Outstanding advances must be cleared before final settlement, as provided in Section 10.4 of NXR-HR-001.

10.5 **Repeat advances.** No new advance will be granted to an employee who has a pending unsettled advance.

## 11. Claims and Reimbursement

11.1 **Submission.** Claims are submitted in NexoraExpense within **15 calendar days** of returning from a trip or of incurring the expense. Each claim must be accompanied by original or scanned itemised receipts and tax invoices for each expense above ₹500. Expenses of ₹500 or below may be claimed without receipts up to a monthly total of ₹3,000.

11.2 **Tax invoices.** Where GST-registered vendors provide invoices, employees should ask for the invoice to be issued in the Company's name with the Company's GSTIN, so that input tax credit can be claimed. The Company's GSTIN is available on NexoraExpense.

11.3 **Approval.** The reporting manager reviews the claim within 5 working days. Claims that require higher-level approval under Section 4 are routed to the Department Head and Finance. Finance verifies the claim against the approved request within 5 working days.

11.4 **Payment.** Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 working days of Finance approval, to the employee's registered bank account. Reimbursements are typically processed on the fifth and twentieth of each month.

11.5 **Late claims.** Claims submitted more than 30 days after the trip ends require the additional approval of the Finance approver. Claims submitted more than 60 days after the trip will not be reimbursed unless the CFO approves an exception in writing.

11.6 **Foreign currency claims.** Employees must convert foreign currency amounts using the card statement, forex receipt, or the Company's month-end rate.

11.7 **Currency and tax treatment.** Reimbursements within the limits in this policy are not treated as taxable perquisites. Payments above the limits, where approved as an exception, may be taxed as a perquisite and processed through payroll.

## 12. Other Business Expenses

12.1 **Communication.** Mobile and data expenses for business use may be reimbursed up to ₹1,200 per month for employees who are required to make frequent business calls, with the manager's approval. Roaming packs for international travel may be claimed with a receipt.

12.2 **Client entertainment.** Client meals and hospitality must be approved in advance by the Department Head, must be reasonable, and must follow the rules on gifts and hospitality in Section 7 of NXR-COC-001. Gifts to clients or business partners with a value above ₹5,000 must also be declared to Legal & Compliance.

12.3 **Training and certification.** Costs for training courses are covered by the annual learning budget of ₹40,000 under Section 8.3 of NXR-HR-001, and not under this policy, unless the course involves travel.

12.4 **Home-office and equipment purchases.** The one-time home-office setup allowance of ₹15,000 is governed by Section 9.2 of NXR-HR-002. Any purchase of equipment, software, or subscriptions must go through IT and the Procurement Policy and must not be claimed as an expense, in accordance with Section 10 of NXR-IT-001.

12.5 **Software and cloud services.** Employees must not purchase software, cloud services, or subscriptions on personal funds or a Company card for use with Company data without IT & Security approval, as stated in Section 10 of NXR-IT-001. Such expenses will not be reimbursed if the tool has not been approved.

12.6 **Company cards.** Corporate credit cards may be issued to employees at M1 grade and above who travel frequently, with the CFO's approval. Cards are for business use only. Personal expenses on the card must be reported and repaid within 15 days.

## 13. Non-reimbursable Expenses, Compliance, and Audit

13.1 **Non-reimbursable items.** The following are not reimbursable:

- Alcohol, tobacco, and recreational drugs.
- Fines, penalties, and traffic violations.
- Personal items, such as clothing, toiletries, and gifts for family.
- Costs of a spouse, family member, or friend who accompanies the employee.
- Loss or theft of personal belongings, unless covered by insurance.
- Entertainment, spa, movies, and minibar items.
- First-class or business-class upgrades not approved under Section 5.3.
- Expenses for which the employee has been reimbursed by a third party.
- Personal expenses on an extended trip.
- Costs of remote work from a different city or country requested by the employee (see Section 2.4).

13.2 **Audit.** Finance conducts a monthly review of a sample of at least 10 percent of all claims and reviews all claims above ₹1,00,000. An annual internal audit is presented to the Audit Committee. Employees must cooperate and provide additional documents if requested.

13.3 **Split transactions.** Finance runs monthly analytics to detect splitting of expenses to avoid approval thresholds, duplicate claims, and unusual patterns. Cases are reported to the CFO, who may refer them to the Ethics Committee under NXR-COC-001.

13.4 **Consequences.** A breach of this policy may result in a claim being rejected, recovery of amounts paid, and disciplinary action under Section 11 of NXR-HR-001. Deliberate falsification is gross misconduct and may result in dismissal and, where appropriate, legal action.

13.5 **Whistleblowing.** Employees who suspect fraudulent claims or misuse of Company funds should report the matter through the channels described in Section 10 of NXR-COC-001 or to ethics@nexoratech.example. Reports are treated confidentially and there will be no retaliation.

## 14. Loss, Theft, and Emergencies

14.1 If a Company laptop, phone, or access token is lost or stolen while travelling, the employee must inform security@nexoratech.example and raise a ticket in NexoraDesk **within one hour** of discovering the loss, in accordance with Section 9 of NXR-IT-001. A police report should be filed for theft, and a copy attached to the incident record.

14.2 In a medical or security emergency, employees should contact the emergency helpline printed on the travel insurance card and the HRBP. The Company will cover reasonable emergency expenses, and approval may be given afterwards.

14.3 Employees should inform their reporting manager and People Operations of any change of travel plans caused by natural disasters, civil unrest, or health advisories. The Company may cancel or recall an employee from a trip for safety reasons, without penalty to the employee.

| CEO | Approve trips above ₹5,00,000 |
