## 1. Purpose

This Human Resources Policy Manual sets out the terms on which Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Nexora", "the Company") employs, develops, rewards, and separates its people. It exists so that every employee, manager, and HR Business Partner (HRBP) works from the same rules, and so that decisions about people are made consistently and fairly across the Pune headquarters, the Bengaluru office, and the Hyderabad office.

Nexora is a software product and services company incorporated in India, with its registered office at Nexora House, Kharadi, Pune 411014, Maharashtra. It employs approximately 640 people across Engineering, Product, IT & Security, Finance, People Operations, Sales, Customer Success, and Legal & Compliance.

This manual is the parent document for people-related policies. It is supported by the following companion documents, which are binding on the same population and must be read together with this manual:

Any unresolved conflict must be raised with the CHRO, who will resolve it together with the document owner concerned.

## 2. Scope and Applicability

2.1 This manual applies to all employees on the payroll of Nexora, regardless of grade, location, or department, including employees on probation, fixed-term contracts, and part-time arrangements.

2.2 Interns, apprentices, and consultants are covered only by those sections that are expressly stated to apply to them, and by their individual engagement letters. Section 14 of this manual (Contractors and Interns) sets out the specific rules that apply to them.

2.3 The Company may amend this manual from time to time. Material changes are approved by the CHRO and the CEO, published on the NexoraHub intranet, and communicated by email at least 15 days before they take effect, except where a change is required by law and must take effect sooner.

2.4 Nothing in this manual creates a contractual right beyond what is stated in the employee's appointment letter. Statutory rights under Indian labour and employment legislation, including the Code on Wages, the Maternity Benefit Act, the Payment of Gratuity Act, and the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013, apply in addition to this manual and prevail in case of inconsistency.

## 3. Employment Categories and Grades

3.1 Nexora recognises the following categories of employment:

- **Permanent employees:** employed without a fixed end date, subject to the probation and notice terms in this manual.
- **Fixed-term employees:** employed for a defined period under a fixed-term appointment letter. Fixed-term employees receive the same statutory benefits as permanent employees, pro-rated where the law permits.
- **Part-time employees:** employed for fewer than 40 hours a week. Leave and benefits are pro-rated to their contracted hours.
- **Interns and apprentices:** engaged for a training period of up to 12 months.

3.2 The grade structure at Nexora is as follows. Grades determine approval authority in several companion policies, including NXR-FIN-001 and NXR-IT-001.

| Grade | Typical Title | Band |
|---|---|---|
| L1 – L3 | Associate, Engineer, Analyst | Individual contributor |
| L4 | Senior Engineer, Senior Analyst | Individual contributor |
| L5 | Lead, Staff Engineer, Principal Analyst | Senior individual contributor |
| M1 | Manager | People leadership |
| M2 | Senior Manager | People leadership |
| D1 | Director / Department Head | Leadership |
| VP | Vice President | Leadership |
| C-level | CEO, CTO, CFO, CHRO, CISO | Executive |

3.3 The term "Department Head" used across all Nexora policies means the most senior person accountable for a department: the Director (D1) or Vice President who leads it. In Engineering, the Department Head is the Vice President of Engineering, Vikram Nair. In Sales, the Department Head is the Vice President of Sales. In Finance, the Department Head is the CFO.

## 4. Recruitment, Onboarding, and Background Verification

4.1 **Hiring approval.** Every new position must be approved on the NexoraPeople system by the requesting Department Head, the Finance business partner (for budget), and the CHRO's delegate in People Operations. Positions at M2 grade and above also require CEO approval.

4.2 **Selection.** Interviews must be structured and documented. At least two interviewers must assess each candidate, and one of them must be from outside the hiring manager's direct team for roles at L4 and above. Hiring decisions must rest on job-related criteria only, consistent with the equal opportunity commitments in NXR-COC-001.

4.3 **Offer and appointment letter.** Offers are issued by People Operations and state the grade, compensation, joining date, probation period, and notice period. The appointment letter is issued on or before the date of joining and must be countersigned by the employee.

4.4 **Background verification.** All offers are conditional on satisfactory verification of identity, education, and the previous two employers. For roles with access to Restricted data (as classified in Section 4 of NXR-IT-001) or with production access, verification also includes a criminal record check to the extent permitted by law. An offer may be withdrawn, or employment terminated, if material misrepresentation is found.

4.5 **Onboarding.** On day one every new joiner receives an employee ID in the format NXR-#### (for example, NXR-1042), a laptop, and a Nexora SSO account with multi-factor authentication. Within the first 10 working days, every new employee must complete the following mandatory modules on the NexoraHub learning platform:

- Information Security Awareness (owned by the CISO, described in Section 13 of NXR-IT-001).
- Code of Conduct and Ethics (owned by Legal & Compliance, described in NXR-COC-001).
- Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) awareness.
- Data Protection Essentials, covering obligations under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Engineering employees additionally complete Secure Development Fundamentals, as required by Section 4 of NXR-ENG-001, before they are granted merge rights on any repository.

## 5. Probation and Confirmation

5.1 **Probation period.** Every new permanent employee serves a probation period of **6 months** from the date of joining. The probation period is stated in the appointment letter.

5.2 **Purpose.** Probation allows the Company and the employee to assess mutual fit. During probation the manager is expected to set clear goals in the first two weeks, hold a one-to-one at least every two weeks, and record written feedback in NexoraPeople at the end of month 2 and month 4.

5.3 **Formal review.** At the start of the fifth month, the reporting manager completes a confirmation assessment. The Department Head and the HRBP review the assessment. The outcome is one of the following:

- **Confirmation:** the employee is confirmed on completion of 6 months. People Operations issues a written confirmation letter within 10 working days of the end of the probation period.
- **Extension:** probation may be extended once, by up to 3 months, where performance or conduct needs improvement. The total probation period, including extension, may not exceed 9 months. The employee must be told the reasons in writing and given a written improvement plan.
- **Non-confirmation:** employment ends with the notice period in Section 5.6.

5.4 **No automatic confirmation.** Continuing to work after the end of the six-month period without a confirmation letter does not constitute confirmation. Managers must complete the assessment on time, and HRBPs will escalate any overdue assessment to the Department Head 10 days before the end of probation.

5.5 **Entitlements during probation.**

- Leave accrues from the date of joining. Casual leave and sick leave (Section 7) may be used during probation. Privileged leave may be taken during probation only with the reporting manager's approval and only up to the accrued balance.
- Employees on probation are covered by group health insurance from day one.
- Employees on probation may work remotely only as permitted by Section 4 of NXR-HR-002 (Remote Work and Hybrid Working Policy). In general, employees in the first three months of probation work from the office unless the Department Head approves an exception.
- Access to production systems for employees on probation is limited to read-only access to masked logs and dashboards, in line with Section 6.3 of NXR-IT-001 and Section 8.2 of NXR-ENG-001. Elevated production access for an employee who has not yet been confirmed requires the joint written approval of the VP of Engineering and the CISO.
- Employees on probation are not eligible for the primary on-call rotation described in Section 9.2 of NXR-ENG-001, and therefore do not receive the on-call allowance in Section 8.4 until confirmed.

5.6 **Notice during probation.** During probation, either party may end the employment by giving **15 calendar days'** written notice, or pay in lieu of notice. The Company may waive the notice period at its discretion. Termination for misconduct follows Section 11.

5.7 **Confirmation and grade changes.** Confirmation does not by itself change grade or compensation. Promotions are handled under Section 9.

## 6. Working Hours and Attendance

6.1 The standard work week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday, based on eight working hours a day plus a one-hour unpaid meal break.

6.2 **Core hours.** All employees, including those working remotely under NXR-HR-002, must be available between 11:00 and 16:00 IST on working days. Meetings involving more than three participants should be scheduled inside core hours.

6.3 **Flexibility.** Outside core hours, start and finish times may be adjusted with the reporting manager's agreement.

6.4 **Attendance.** Attendance is recorded on NexoraPeople. Hybrid employees must mark the location of work each day, whether office or remote. Three unexplained absences in a calendar month must be reported by the manager to the HRBP.

6.5 **Overtime and compensatory off.** Employees at grade L1 to L5 who are required by their manager to work on a weekly off or a public holiday are entitled to one day of compensatory off for each such day, to be taken within 60 days. Managers (M1 and above) are not entitled to compensatory off except under Section 8.4 for incident response.

6.6 **Public holidays.** The Company observes 12 public holidays each calendar year, published on NexoraHub every December for the following year. Locations may vary in up to three holidays to reflect regional festivals.

## 7. Leave

7.1 **Leave entitlement.** Employees are entitled to the following leave in each calendar year. Entitlements are pro-rated for joiners in the year of joining.

| Leave Type | Entitlement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Privileged Leave (PL) | 18 working days | Accrues at 1.5 days per month |
| Casual Leave (CL) | 8 working days | Maximum 3 consecutive days |
| Sick Leave (SL) | 8 working days | Medical certificate needed for more than 2 consecutive days |
| Maternity Leave | 26 weeks | For the first two children; as per the Maternity Benefit Act |
| Paternity Leave | 10 working days | To be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption |
| Bereavement Leave | 5 working days | Immediate family |
| Marriage Leave | 5 working days | Once during employment |

7.2 **Carry forward and encashment.** Up to 12 days of unused PL may be carried forward to the next year. The maximum PL balance is 30 days. On separation, an employee's unused PL balance is encashed, up to the maximum balance of 30 days, at the rate of basic salary divided by 26 days. Casual leave and sick leave lapse at year end.

7.3 **Applying for leave.** Leave must be applied for on NexoraPeople and approved by the reporting manager. Planned leave of five or more consecutive working days should be applied for at least 15 days in advance. Sick leave should be reported to the manager on the first day of absence.

7.4 **Leave during notice period.** PL may be taken during a notice period only with the approval of the Department Head, and the notice period is extended by the number of days of leave taken, except for statutory leave.

7.5 **Business travel and leave.** Time spent on approved business travel under NXR-FIN-001 is treated as working time. Travel on a weekend or public holiday at the Company's request qualifies for compensatory off under Section 6.5.

## 8. Compensation and Benefits

8.1 **Pay structure.** Compensation consists of fixed pay (basic salary, house rent allowance, and special allowance), an annual performance bonus for eligible grades, and, for selected roles, long-term incentives. Compensation is expressed as annual Cost to Company (CTC) in Indian rupees.

8.2 **Payroll.** Salary is paid on the last working day of each month by direct credit to the employee's registered bank account. Statutory deductions, including Provident Fund, Professional Tax, and Tax Deducted at Source, are made in accordance with law. Payslips are available on NexoraPeople.

8.3 **Benefits.** All confirmed and probationary employees are eligible for:

- Group health insurance with a cover of ₹5,00,000 per family per year, covering the employee, spouse, and up to two dependent children. Parents may be added at the employee's cost.
- Group personal accident insurance and group term life insurance.
- Provident Fund at the statutory rate.
- Gratuity in accordance with the Payment of Gratuity Act, after five years of continuous service.
- A one-time home-office setup allowance of ₹15,000 for approved remote and hybrid employees, as further described in Section 9 of NXR-HR-002.
- An annual learning budget of ₹40,000 per employee, approved by the reporting manager.

8.4 **On-call allowance.** Confirmed employees who serve in a formal on-call rotation, as defined in Section 9 of NXR-ENG-001, receive an on-call allowance of **₹8,000 per week** of primary on-call duty and ₹3,000 per week of secondary on-call duty. An employee who is actively engaged for more than three hours on a SEV1 or SEV2 incident (as defined in Section 10 of NXR-ENG-001) outside working hours receives one day of compensatory off, irrespective of grade.

8.5 **Referral bonus.** An employee who refers a candidate who joins Nexora receives a referral bonus of ₹30,000 for Engineering roles and ₹15,000 for all other roles. The bonus is payable after the referred employee has been **confirmed**, that is, after the successful completion of the 6-month probation period, and if both employees remain in service on the payment date.

8.6 **Expense reimbursement.** Business expenses, including domestic and international travel, are reimbursed only in accordance with NXR-FIN-001. Employees must not use personal funds for purchases above ₹25,000 on the assumption that they will be reimbursed; such spending requires prior approval as set out in Section 4 of NXR-FIN-001.

8.7 **Salary review.** Salary is reviewed once a year in April, based on performance outcomes and market benchmarking. Salary revisions take effect from 1 April. Employees who are on probation on 1 April are eligible for review only after confirmation.

## 9. Performance Management and Development

9.1 **Performance cycles.** Nexora runs two performance cycles a year: April to September (H1) and October to March (H2). Goals are set at the start of each cycle using the Objectives and Key Results (OKR) framework on NexoraPeople.

9.2 **Rating scale.** Employees are rated on a five-point scale: 1 (Needs Significant Improvement), 2 (Partially Meets), 3 (Meets Expectations), 4 (Exceeds Expectations), and 5 (Outstanding). Ratings are calibrated by Department Heads and approved by the CHRO. The distribution is monitored for consistency but is not forced.

9.3 **Feedback.** Managers hold a one-to-one at least monthly with each direct report and document the mid-cycle conversation.

9.4 **Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).** An employee who receives a rating of 1 in either cycle, or a rating of 2 in two consecutive cycles, may be placed on a PIP of 60 to 90 days. The PIP must set out measurable expectations, support to be provided, and the consequences of not meeting expectations. The HRBP must be involved from the outset.

9.5 **Promotions.** Promotions are considered once a year. A candidate must be confirmed, must have a rating of 4 or above in at least one of the last two cycles, and must demonstrate performance at the next grade. Promotions to M1 and above require the Department Head's and the CHRO's approval, and promotions to D1 and above require the CEO's approval.

9.6 **Learning and development.** Each employee is encouraged to complete at least 24 hours of learning per year. Managers are accountable for ensuring that annual mandatory compliance training, including Information Security Awareness under NXR-IT-001 and Code of Conduct refreshers under NXR-COC-001, is completed by the last working day of June each year.

## 10. Separation

10.1 **Resignation.** An employee wishing to resign must submit a written resignation on NexoraPeople. The reporting manager and the HRBP will hold an exit conversation within five working days.

10.2 **Notice periods.** After confirmation, the notice period is **60 calendar days** for employees at grades L1 to L5 and **90 calendar days** for employees at grade M1 and above. The notice period during probation is 15 calendar days (see Section 5.6). The Company may accept a shorter notice period, or waive notice, at its discretion. Notice buy-out is available with the approval of the Department Head and the CHRO, and is calculated on the basis of fixed pay for the shortfall days.

10.3 **Knowledge transfer.** Employees serving notice must complete a documented handover. Engineering employees must hand over runbooks, on-call responsibilities, and secrets ownership in the manner set out in Sections 9 and 11 of NXR-ENG-001.

10.4 **Exit clearance and access revocation.** On the last working day, People Operations, IT, Finance, and Admin must complete clearance. IT must revoke all access, including Nexora SSO, NexoraVPN, and any production access held through Nexora Access Vault, by 18:00 IST on the last working day, and in the case of involuntary termination for cause, immediately on notification, as required by Section 12 of NXR-IT-001. Any outstanding travel advances under NXR-FIN-001 must be settled before the final settlement.

10.5 **Full and final settlement.** Full and final settlement, including salary up to the last working day, leave encashment, and gratuity where applicable, is paid within 45 days of the last working day, subject to completion of clearance.

10.6 **Post-employment obligations.** Confidentiality, intellectual property, and non-solicitation obligations survive termination for the periods set out in the appointment letter and in NXR-COC-001.

## 11. Disciplinary Procedure

11.1 Employees are expected to comply with this manual, the Code of Conduct and Ethics (NXR-COC-001), the IT Security Policy (NXR-IT-001), and all other Company policies. Breaches may lead to disciplinary action.

11.2 **Types of action.** Depending on the seriousness and circumstances, action may include:

1. Verbal counselling, documented by the manager.
2. Written warning.
3. Final written warning.
4. Suspension with or without pay, pending or following investigation, as permitted by law.
5. Demotion or withholding of a salary revision or bonus.
6. Termination of employment.

11.3 **Process.** Except where an act of gross misconduct calls for immediate action, the employee is given written notice of the allegation, a fair opportunity to respond, and the right to be accompanied by a colleague at any disciplinary meeting. Investigations are led by the HRBP or, for matters covered by NXR-COC-001, by the Ethics Committee. Cases involving allegations of sexual harassment are referred exclusively to the Internal Committee as described in Section 5 of NXR-COC-001.

11.4 **Gross misconduct.** Examples include theft, fraud, falsification of records, serious breach of information security (for example, sharing credentials, disabling endpoint protection, or accessing production systems without authorisation contrary to NXR-IT-001 Section 6), sexual harassment, violence, and deliberate breach of confidentiality. Gross misconduct may result in summary dismissal after due inquiry.

11.5 **Records.** Disciplinary records are stored on NexoraPeople with restricted access. Warnings remain active for 12 months unless stated otherwise.

## 12. Grievance Redressal

12.1 Employees who have a concern about work, treatment, or pay are encouraged first to raise it with their reporting manager.

12.2 If the concern is not resolved, or the employee is not comfortable raising it with the manager, the employee may submit a formal grievance to their HRBP through NexoraDesk or by email to people@nexoratech.example. The HRBP will acknowledge the grievance within 2 working days and respond in writing within 10 working days.

12.3 If the employee is not satisfied with the outcome, the employee may appeal to the CHRO within 5 working days of receiving the response. The CHRO will decide within 15 working days, and the CHRO's decision is final within the internal process.

12.4 Grievances involving harassment, discrimination, fraud, or retaliation are handled under NXR-COC-001, and may be reported anonymously through the ethics helpline at ethics@nexoratech.example.

12.5 No employee shall suffer retaliation for raising a grievance in good faith.

## 13. Employee Records and Privacy

13.1 People Operations maintains employee records on NexoraPeople. These records include personal data and are classified as Restricted under NXR-IT-001.

13.2 Access to employee records is limited to People Operations, the employee's reporting chain (limited to what is needed for management), and Finance for payroll.

13.3 Nexora processes employee personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and Section 9 of NXR-COC-001. Requests to access or correct personal data may be made to the Data Protection Officer, Nisha Patil, at dpo@nexoratech.example.

13.4 Employment records are retained for at least eight years after separation, or for longer where required by law.

## 14. Contractors and Interns

14.1 Interns and contractors are not employees and are not entitled to leave, probation, or benefits under this manual except as stated in their engagement letters.

14.2 Interns are engaged for up to 12 months with a monthly stipend. An intern who is offered a permanent role begins a fresh probation period of 6 months, unless the CHRO approves a shorter period in writing.

14.3 Contractors and interns must comply with NXR-IT-001, NXR-COC-001, and, where relevant, NXR-ENG-001. Contractors and interns are never granted production write access. Read-only access may be granted with the approval of the Department Head and the CISO for a maximum period defined in Section 6.3 of NXR-IT-001.

14.4 Contractors may be reimbursed for pre-approved travel only under the terms of the contract and the rules in NXR-FIN-001, and travel above ₹25,000 for a contractor requires the same Department Head and Finance approvals as for an employee.
