## 1. Purpose

Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Nexora", "the Company") believes that thoughtful flexibility helps people do their best work, while time together in the office builds trust, shared context, and better products. This policy defines how employees may work away from a Nexora office, who must approve it, and what security, availability, and equipment conditions apply.

This policy is issued under, and must be read with, the Human Resources Policy Manual (NXR-HR-001). It also relies on the Information Technology Security Policy (NXR-IT-001), which sets the mandatory technical controls for remote working, and on the Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy (NXR-FIN-001), which governs which costs the Company will and will not reimburse.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This policy applies to all Nexora employees at the Pune headquarters (Nexora House, Kharadi), the Bengaluru office, and the Hyderabad office, including employees on probation.

2.2 It also applies, with the modifications in Section 14 of NXR-HR-001, to contractors and interns who are permitted to work remotely under their engagement terms.

2.3 It does not apply to business travel, which is governed by NXR-FIN-001. An employee who is travelling on approved business travel and works from a hotel or client site is not working remotely under this policy, but must still comply with the security requirements in Section 6 of this policy.

## 3. Definitions and Working Models

3.1 **Office-based.** The employee works from a Nexora office on all working days. Roles that require physical presence, such as hardware lab technicians, facilities staff, and IT desk support, are office-based.

3.2 **Hybrid.** The default model for most roles. A hybrid employee works from the office for **at least three days a week** and may work remotely for up to two days a week, subject to approval under Section 5.

3.3 **Fully remote.** An employee whose role has been formally designated as remote-eligible works from an approved home location and visits the office only for planned events. Fully remote status is an exception, not the norm, and is approved as described in Section 5.4.

3.4 **Ad hoc remote work.** Occasional remote days that fall outside the employee's regular pattern, for example to wait for a repair visit or to attend to a personal matter.

3.5 **Remote work location.** The employee's registered home address on NexoraPeople, in the same city as the employee's assigned Nexora office. Work from any other location is governed by Sections 7 and 8.

3.6 **Approved device.** A laptop issued and managed by Nexora IT, with disk encryption, endpoint protection, and the Nexora device management agent installed, as required by Section 7 of NXR-IT-001.

## 4. Eligibility

4.1 **General rule.** Any employee whose role is capable of being performed remotely, as determined by their reporting manager and Department Head, may apply for hybrid working.

4.2 **Employees on probation.** Every new employee serves a probation period of 6 months under Section 5 of NXR-HR-001. Because the early months are important for learning, building relationships, and receiving close feedback:

- During the **first three months of probation**, employees work from the office on all working days, unless the Department Head approves an exception in writing, for example because the team is distributed across locations.
- From month four until confirmation, employees may follow the hybrid model with the approval of the reporting manager in accordance with Section 5.
- Employees whose probation has been extended under Section 5.3 of NXR-HR-001 revert to the office-only pattern until the reporting manager confirms that performance has recovered.

4.3 **Employees under a performance improvement plan.** Employees on a Performance Improvement Plan under Section 9.4 of NXR-HR-001 may be required to work from the office for the duration of the plan, at the discretion of the Department Head and the HRBP.

4.4 **Interns and apprentices.** Interns work from the office unless their engagement letter says otherwise. Interns are never permitted to work from a location outside India.

4.5 **Role suitability.** A manager may decline a remote work request if the role requires physical presence, if customer or business needs require in-person collaboration, or if the employee does not meet the conditions in this policy. The manager must record the reason on NexoraPeople if the employee asks for it.

4.6 **Ineligibility for security reasons.** Employees who have an open information security violation under NXR-IT-001 or an open disciplinary process under Section 11 of NXR-HR-001 are not eligible for remote work until the matter has been closed.

## 5. Approval Process

5.1 **Remote work requires manager approval and a working VPN.** No employee may work remotely, whether for one day or on a regular basis, without the prior approval of their reporting manager, and without the ability to connect to NexoraVPN on an approved device as set out in Section 6. Both conditions must be met at all times.

5.2 **Regular hybrid arrangement.** The employee submits a request on NexoraPeople stating the proposed remote days, home address, and the workspace arrangement. The reporting manager approves or declines within 5 working days. The approved pattern is recorded on NexoraPeople and reviewed at each performance cycle under Section 9 of NXR-HR-001. The HRBP is notified automatically but does not need to approve.

5.3 **Ad hoc remote days.** Ad hoc remote days must be requested through NexoraPeople as early as possible, and no later than the start of the working day. Approval can be given by the reporting manager by message or on the system. No more than **5 ad hoc remote days** in a calendar month are permitted without the Department Head's approval.

5.4 **Fully remote designation.** A role may be designated fully remote only if all of the following approvals are recorded:

1. The reporting manager, who confirms that the role can be performed remotely.
2. The Department Head, who confirms that the designation is appropriate for the team and the business.
3. The HRBP, who confirms that the employee is confirmed (post-probation) and has no open disciplinary matter.
4. The CISO's delegate in IT & Security, who confirms that the employee's home location can meet the security requirements in Section 6.

Fully remote designations are reviewed every 12 months.

5.5 **Approval is discretionary and revocable.** Approval does not create a right. The Company may withdraw or vary an arrangement in accordance with Section 12.

5.6 **Team anchor days.** Departments may designate up to two days a week as team anchor days, on which every member of a team is expected to attend in person. The Department Head decides the anchor days and communicates them at least 30 days before they take effect. A remote work approval never overrides a team anchor day unless the reporting manager explicitly agrees to an exception.

5.7 **Approval summary.**

| Arrangement | Approver | Additional Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Regular hybrid pattern | Reporting manager | VPN and approved device |
| Ad hoc remote day (up to 5 per month) | Reporting manager | VPN and approved device |
| More than 5 ad hoc days per month | Department Head | VPN and approved device |
| Remote work in first 3 months of probation | Department Head | VPN and approved device |
| Fully remote designation | Reporting manager, Department Head, HRBP, IT & Security delegate | Employee confirmed |
| Remote work over 30 days from a different city | Department Head and HRBP | IT & Security informed |
| Work from abroad | CHRO, CISO, Head of Legal & Compliance | Maximum 30 calendar days per year |

## 6. Security Requirements for Remote Work

6.1 **Mandatory controls.** All remote work is subject to the controls below, which implement Sections 7 and 8 of NXR-IT-001. Failure to comply is treated as a breach of information security and may lead to disciplinary action under Section 11 of NXR-HR-001.

6.2 **VPN.** Employees must connect to **NexoraVPN** before accessing any system classified as Internal, Confidential, or Restricted under Section 4 of NXR-IT-001. This includes email, source code repositories, the internal issue tracker, NexoraPeople, and any customer data. The VPN client must remain connected for the entire working session. Split tunnelling is disabled and must not be circumvented.

6.3 **Approved devices only.** Remote work must be done on an approved device. Personal laptops, tablets, and mobile phones must not be used to access source code, customer data, or Restricted information. Access to email and calendar on personal mobile phones is permitted only through the Nexora-approved mobile application management profile.

6.4 **Multi-factor authentication.** Every login to Nexora SSO from a remote location requires multi-factor authentication. Authentication codes and hardware tokens must not be shared.

6.5 **Network use.** Employees should use a private, password-protected home network with WPA2 or WPA3 encryption and a router password that is not the factory default. Public Wi-Fi networks, such as those in cafes and airports, may be used only with NexoraVPN connected. Public charging kiosks must not be used with company devices.

6.6 **Physical security.** The workspace must be arranged so that family members, visitors, and members of the public cannot read the screen or overhear calls involving Confidential or Restricted information. Company devices must be locked when unattended, and the screen lock must activate after 5 minutes of inactivity. Laptops must never be left in a vehicle.

6.7 **Printing and storage.** Employees must not print Restricted information at home and must not store Company data on personal cloud storage, personal email, or removable media. Company documents must remain in the Company's approved document repositories.

6.8 **Production access from remote locations.** Access to production systems from a remote location is permitted only where all of the following are true: the employee is connected to NexoraVPN, uses an approved device, has completed multi-factor authentication, and has obtained a time-bound access grant through Nexora Access Vault under Section 6 of NXR-IT-001 and Section 8 of NXR-ENG-001. Employees on probation have read-only access only, as set out in Section 6.3 of NXR-IT-001. Production access is never permitted from a public or shared computer, or from a location outside India except as approved under Section 8 of this policy.

6.9 **Reporting incidents.** If a device is lost or stolen, or if the employee suspects that credentials have been compromised, the employee must report it to security@nexoratech.example and through NexoraDesk **within one hour** of becoming aware, as required by Section 9 of NXR-IT-001, whether or not it is a working day.

6.10 **Audit and monitoring.** The Company monitors VPN connections, device compliance, and access to sensitive systems in accordance with Section 11 of NXR-IT-001. Remote employees consent to such monitoring as a condition of remote work.

## 7. Work from Another Location Within India

7.1 **Approval.** An employee who wishes to work from a location in India other than their registered home address, for example while visiting family, must obtain their reporting manager's approval under Section 5, and the location must meet the security requirements in Section 6.

7.2 **Up to 30 days.** Remote work from a location in a different city is permitted for a **maximum of 30 calendar days** in a calendar year with the reporting manager's approval. Days worked from a different city count towards the employee's hybrid pattern, and the employee must still be available during core hours of 11:00 to 16:00 IST as set out in Section 6.2 of NXR-HR-001.

7.3 **Over 30 days.** Any period that would take the employee's total remote work from a different city beyond 30 calendar days in a calendar year requires the joint approval of the Department Head and the HRBP, with IT & Security informed. The Company may decline such a request because of tax, labour law, or business considerations, and may require the employee to relocate formally under the transfer process in NXR-HR-001.

7.4 **Costs.** Remote work from a different city is at the employee's own cost. The Company does not reimburse travel to or from the alternative location, accommodation, or the cost of moving equipment. NXR-FIN-001 applies only to business travel that the Company has required, and does not apply to remote work in another city.

7.5 **Relocation.** An employee who permanently changes home city must update their address on NexoraPeople and request a formal transfer. Continuing to work remotely from the new city without an approved transfer is a breach of this policy.

## 8. Work from Abroad

8.1 **General rule.** Working from outside India creates tax, immigration, data protection, and labour law obligations for both the employee and the Company. Employees must not work from outside India, other than on approved business travel under NXR-FIN-001, without prior written approval under this section.

8.2 **Approval.** Work from abroad requires the written approval of all three of the following, in addition to the reporting manager and the Department Head:

- The **CHRO**, who considers the employee's record and business need.
- The **CISO**, who considers information security, data residency, and device risk.
- The **Head of Legal & Compliance**, Ananya Bhatt, who considers tax, permanent establishment, immigration, and data protection.

8.3 **Duration.** Approved work from abroad is limited to a **maximum of 30 calendar days in any 12-month period**, and to countries on the approved list maintained by Legal & Compliance. Requests must be submitted at least 30 days in advance.

8.4 **Restrictions.** Employees who are on probation, on a Performance Improvement Plan, or working in roles with access to Restricted data or production write access are not eligible for work from abroad, unless the CEO approves an exception on the joint recommendation of the CISO and the CHRO.

8.5 **Access controls.** IT & Security may apply geographic access controls. Production access from outside India is disabled by default and is enabled only where the CISO has approved it as part of the work-from-abroad approval, for a defined period, under Section 14 of NXR-IT-001.

8.6 **Costs.** The employee bears the cost of travel, accommodation, and any tax or immigration consequences that arise from a request initiated by the employee. If the Company asks an employee to work abroad, the arrangement is treated as international business travel under Section 9 of NXR-FIN-001, and requires Department Head and CFO approval.

## 9. Equipment, Allowances, and Workspace

9.1 **Laptop and peripherals.** Every employee receives a Company laptop. Hybrid employees may request one external monitor, keyboard, and mouse for their home workspace from IT, subject to the reporting manager's approval.

9.2 **Home-office setup allowance.** An employee whose hybrid or fully remote arrangement has been approved is eligible for a one-time home-office setup allowance of **₹15,000**, which can be used for furniture such as a chair or desk, lighting, or accessories. The allowance is claimed through NexoraExpense, with receipts, within 60 days of approval. It is available once per employee, and is separate from the equipment provided by IT. Purchases above ₹25,000 for a single item require prior approval under Section 4 of NXR-FIN-001.

9.3 **Connectivity allowance.** Hybrid and fully remote employees receive a monthly connectivity allowance of **₹1,000**, paid with salary, to defray broadband costs. The allowance is not paid during months in which the employee works remotely for fewer than 6 days.

9.4 **Ownership and return.** All equipment provided by the Company remains the property of Nexora. Equipment must be returned on separation as part of the exit clearance described in Section 10.4 of NXR-HR-001. Furniture bought with the setup allowance remains the property of the employee.

9.5 **Repairs and support.** Employees can raise hardware and software issues through NexoraDesk. IT will repair or replace faulty devices. Employees must not attempt to repair Company laptops themselves or take them to third-party repair shops.

9.6 **Safety and ergonomics.** Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe and ergonomic workspace. The Company provides an ergonomic self-assessment checklist on NexoraHub. Work-related injuries at home should be reported to the HRBP.

## 10. Availability, Performance, and Communication

10.1 **Core hours.** Remote employees must be available and responsive between 11:00 and 16:00 IST on working days, in line with Section 6.2 of NXR-HR-001, and must be reachable on the Company's messaging platform and email.

10.2 **Working hours.** The standard 40-hour week applies. Remote working does not change an employee's entitlement to leave under Section 7 of NXR-HR-001. Employees should not work when on approved leave, and managers should not expect responses from employees on leave.

10.3 **Outcome-based management.** Managers assess remote employees by outcomes, quality, and collaboration, not by hours of visible activity. Managers must apply the same standards to remote and office-based employees in the performance cycles under Section 9 of NXR-HR-001.

10.4 **Meetings.** Team meetings must use the Company's approved video conferencing platform. Cameras should be on for one-to-ones and team meetings unless there is a good reason, such as bandwidth. Recordings must not be made without the consent of participants and must follow Section 8 of NXR-COC-001 on confidentiality.

10.5 **On-call employees.** Employees who are on the on-call rotation under Section 9 of NXR-ENG-001 must be able to acknowledge a page within the timelines specified there. On-call employees who work remotely must have a stable network connection and a backup connectivity option, such as a mobile hotspot. The Company will reimburse reasonable costs for a backup connection up to ₹1,000 per month for on-call employees, through NexoraExpense.

10.6 **Attendance recording.** Employees must record on NexoraPeople whether each working day is worked in the office or remotely. Recording the wrong location knowingly is a breach of the Code of Conduct (NXR-COC-001).

## 11. Health, Wellbeing, and Inclusion

11.1 Managers should check in regularly with remote employees, watch for signs of isolation or overwork, and encourage the use of leave.

11.2 Employees who are struggling with remote working are encouraged to speak to their manager or HRBP. The Company's Employee Assistance Programme provides free and confidential counselling to all employees and their families.

11.3 Remote employees must be given equal access to opportunities, including promotions under Section 9.5 of NXR-HR-001, projects, and development. Managers must not treat remote employees less favourably because of their working arrangement.

## 12. Changing or Ending a Remote Arrangement

12.1 **Company-initiated changes.** The Company may vary or withdraw a remote arrangement with 15 days' written notice if any of the following applies: business or customer needs change; performance or conduct concerns arise; the employee breaches this policy or NXR-IT-001; the team's anchor days change; or the employee's role changes. In urgent cases, such as a security concern, the arrangement may be suspended immediately by the CISO or the Department Head.

12.2 **Employee-initiated changes.** An employee who wishes to change their pattern must submit a new request under Section 5.

12.3 **Suspension for security reasons.** The CISO may suspend an employee's remote access if there is a reasonable suspicion of compromise or a policy breach. Suspension continues until IT & Security has completed its review.

12.4 **Separation.** During the notice period, the Department Head may require an employee to work from the office to support handover, in line with Section 10.3 of NXR-HR-001.

## 13. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Employee | Follow this policy, keep VPN and device compliant, record work location, report incidents within one hour |
| Reporting manager | Approve or decline requests, manage by outcomes, ensure the employee meets the requirements |
| Department Head | Approve exceptions, set team anchor days, approve fully remote roles and long-duration requests |
| HRBP | Advise, review long-duration requests, monitor consistency |
| IT & Security | Maintain VPN and device management, advise on location risk, monitor compliance |
| CISO | Co-approve work from abroad, suspend access where necessary |
| Head of Legal & Compliance | Advise on tax, immigration, and data protection risks |
| CHRO | Own this policy, approve work from abroad |

## 14. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be requested in writing through NexoraDesk and must be approved by the CHRO. Exceptions that relate to technical controls in Section 6 must also be approved by the CISO under Section 14 of NXR-IT-001. All exceptions are time-bound and are recorded in the exceptions register maintained by People Operations.
